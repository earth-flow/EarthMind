IBM Elite Support offers enterprise support for EarthMind to help you develop and deploy EarthMind applications with confidence.

IBM Elite Support for EarthMind is a subscription to the EarthMind expertise at IBM.
It's meant for EarthMind users who want all the benefits of running their own open-source deployments, as well as the peace of mind that comes with having direct access to the team that has authored the majority of the EarthMind code.

IBM Elite Support subscribers can get help with general-purpose and technical questions for their open-source EarthMind deployments.
If an issue is encountered, IBM is there to help.

To subscribe or learn more, see [IBM Elite Support for EarthMind](https://ibm.com/docs/esfl?topic=elite-support-earthmind-specification).
